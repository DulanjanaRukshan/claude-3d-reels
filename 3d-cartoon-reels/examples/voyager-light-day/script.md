Facts (NASA / JPL, Nov 18 2026): Voyager 1 reached one light-day (about 25.9 billion km / 16.1 billion miles) at 5:16 a.m. ET on Nov 18 2026, 49 years 2 months 11 days after launch on Sept 5 1977. Speed is about 38,000 mph (≈61,000 km/h), so one light-year would take about 17,600 years. It left the heliosphere in 2012. Voyager 2 is expected to reach one light-day in November 2035.
